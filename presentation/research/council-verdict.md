# Council verdict

Composition: Ada (formal correctness), Aristotle (structure and categories), Feynman (mechanism and first-principles explanation).
Protocol: independent multi-agent analysis, peer cross-examination, final positions. All three read the paper and actual solver sources. Main agent generated fresh numerical evidence.

Consensus: research-led opening, an explicitly labeled ideal 1D teaching example, an early compact nested map, and a detailed optical feedback sweep before expanding the V-cycle, PCG, and outer loop. Present noisy 2D results separately. Put the full matrix atlas and detailed recurrences in the appendix.

Disagreement: Feynman initially preferred bottom-up explanation; Aristotle initially preferred the full top-down recurrence. Reconciliation retains a small orientation map early, then explains the optical leaf before the full solver. Ada favors fewer main slides and warns against spending hardware-explanation time on familiar PCG algebra.

Minority concern: compression must not omit control-flow steps or make PCG a black box. Resolve with progressive flowchart pages and a complete appendix, rather than one crowded diagram.

Important findings: main.py's coarse solve is 704×704 in this run; its cost is omitted from fine-grid MVM counters. The browser counts transfers and zero-input products, unlike main.py. All matrices are fixed during solving, but CPU and optical operations may use the same A. Original nonzero boundary values imply zero boundary changes for correction problems.

Unresolved hardware questions: signed encoding, sparse tiling, physical routing, calibration, conversion/memory overhead, and variable-preconditioner theory. Do not claim these are implemented or experimentally validated.
